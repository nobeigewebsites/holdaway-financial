Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled when you visit this website or contact Holdaway Financial Services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Holdaway Financial Services respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information may be handled when you visit this website or contact Jonathan Holdaway using the contact details provided on it.

2. Information you may provide

This website does not currently use an online enquiry form. If you choose to contact Jonathan directly by email or telephone, you may provide personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your location
  • Information contained within your enquiry

Please avoid sending sensitive financial information through an initial email unless Jonathan has asked you to do so using an appropriate method.

3. How information may be used

Information provided when you contact Holdaway Financial Services may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Arrange an initial conversation or meeting
  • Understand the nature of the help you are seeking
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory or professional obligations where relevant

4. Contacting Holdaway Financial Services

When you use an email link on this website, your message is sent using your own email provider or email application. The website itself does not receive or store the contents of that email.

Once your message is received by Holdaway Financial Services, the information contained within it will be handled in connection with your enquiry.

5. Telephone enquiries

Telephone numbers displayed on this website are provided so that visitors can contact Jonathan directly.

Calls made using the telephone links on this website are handled by your telephone provider and are not processed through the website.

6. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

As with most website hosting services, technical information relating to visits to the website may be processed by the hosting provider for purposes such as security, reliability and operation of the service.

Further information about cookies and similar technologies is available in the Cookie Policy.

7. External websites

This website contains links to external organisations and resources, including government and regulatory websites.

Holdaway Financial Services is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. When you follow an external link, the privacy policy of that website will apply.

8. Sharing personal information

Personal information will not be sold to third parties.

Information may need to be shared with relevant service providers, professional organisations or other parties where this is necessary to provide services, comply with legal or regulatory obligations, or where you have authorised the sharing of information.

9. Keeping information secure

Reasonable steps are taken to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

No method of electronic communication or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so visitors should avoid sending confidential financial information through ordinary email unless specifically requested.

10. How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet any applicable legal, regulatory or professional record-keeping requirements.

11. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable data protection law, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of information and, in some circumstances, to object to processing.

Some rights are subject to legal and regulatory exceptions, including requirements that certain financial records must be retained.

13. Complaints

If you have concerns about the way your personal information has been handled, please contact Holdaway Financial Services in the first instance.

You may also have the right to raise a complaint with the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

14. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, legal requirements or the way personal information is handled.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last updated.
