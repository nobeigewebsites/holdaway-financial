Information about cookies and similar technologies used when you visit this website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

They are commonly used to help websites function correctly, remember preferences, improve security or understand how visitors use a site.

2. Cookies used on this website

This website has been designed to operate without the use of advertising cookies, marketing trackers or visitor profiling tools.

Holdaway Financial Services does not currently use tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar advertising and behavioural tracking services on this website.

3. Essential and technical cookies

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

The hosting platform or other technical services involved in delivering the website may use essential technologies or process technical information necessary for security, reliability, fraud prevention and operation of the service.

These technologies are controlled by the relevant service provider rather than Holdaway Financial Services.

4. Third-party websites

This website contains links to third-party websites, including government, regulatory and other external resources.

When you follow one of these links, the external website may use its own cookies or tracking technologies.

Those cookies are governed by the privacy and cookie policies of the external website and are outside the control of Holdaway Financial Services.

5. Email and telephone links

Clicking an email link on this website opens your own email application or email service.

Clicking a telephone number on a compatible device allows your device to begin a telephone call.

These actions are handled by your own device or service provider and do not place marketing cookies on this website.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

Most internet browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their settings.

You can usually control cookies through your browser's privacy or security settings. The exact steps depend on the browser and device you use.

Blocking all cookies may affect the way some websites work, although this website is designed to function without non-essential tracking cookies.

7. Cookie consent banner

Because this website does not currently set non-essential advertising, analytics or behavioural tracking cookies, a cookie consent banner is not currently used.

If additional technologies are added in future — such as website analytics, advertising tools, embedded marketing services or other non-essential cookies — this policy and the website's consent arrangements will be reviewed.

8. Changes to this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if the technologies used by the website change or if applicable legal requirements change.

The date shown at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last updated.
