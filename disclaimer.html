1. General information only

The information provided on this website is intended for general information and educational purposes only.

Nothing on this website should be treated as personalised financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, legal advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any particular investment, pension, financial product or service.

2. Your individual circumstances matter

Financial planning depends on individual circumstances, objectives, attitude to risk, financial position and other relevant factors.

Information that may be appropriate for one person may not be appropriate for another.

Personal financial decisions should therefore be made only after considering your own circumstances and, where appropriate, receiving personalised professional advice.

3. Investments involve risk

The value of investments can fall as well as rise and you may not get back the amount originally invested.

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance.

Investment returns are not guaranteed and different types of investment involve different levels and types of risk.

4. Pensions and retirement planning

Pension and retirement decisions can have significant long-term consequences.

The suitability of a pension strategy depends on individual circumstances, objectives, existing arrangements and applicable legislation.

Information on this website relating to pensions or retirement should not be treated as a personalised recommendation.

5. Tax

Tax treatment depends on individual circumstances and may change in the future.

Tax rules can also vary between countries and jurisdictions.

Any tax-related information appearing on this website is general in nature and should not be relied upon as individual tax advice.

6. International and expat planning

Financial planning for people living or working internationally can involve more than one legal, tax and regulatory jurisdiction.

The availability of particular services or financial products may depend on where a person is resident, their individual circumstances and the regulatory permissions applicable to the service being provided.

The presence of information on this website does not mean that every service described is available in every country.
