Stage 2

Implementation if needed

Putting recommendations
into place.

If you and Jonathan agree that financial products should be arranged — such as investments, pensions or protection — this becomes a regulated advice process and separate fees apply.

These are charged as a percentage of funds invested and reduce as investments grow.

You'll receive a clear illustration before proceeding so you know exactly what to expect.

Implementation fees cover

  • Research
  • Recommendations
  • Implementation
  • Ongoing advice and servicing

Family members investing together may benefit from lower overall fees.
